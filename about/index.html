About Us

CFOLogic is your strategic navigator.

CFOLogic is a global finance services firm that works alongside the Office of the CFO to help fast-growing small and mid-sized businesses build scalable and disciplined finance functions. We partner closely with CEOs, CFOs, and Finance Leaders to strengthen financial operations, improve reporting reliability, and enable better decision-making through structured processes and financial intelligence.

Our role goes beyond traditional finance outsourcing. We support leadership teams in building a finance function that can keep pace with growth - combining operational execution, analytical insight, and governance frameworks that improve visibility, control, and strategic alignment across the organization.
