How we price

A fee you can predict, built from the actual work.

Every provider on this page prices from the same handful of variables. Most of them do not tell you which. Here are ours, and the three steps that turn them into a number.

01

Discovery call

Thirty minutes on what you sell, how money moves and what you need to see each month. No document request list before we have understood the business.

02

Scope review

We look at your bank and card accounts, payroll, how far behind the books are, and the reports you actually use. The scope comes out of that, not out of a template.

03

A fixed monthly fee

One number, agreed before you start, with what is in and what is out written down. Filings and add-ons are priced separately so you only pay for what you file.

What moves the number

  • Transaction volumeThe count of bank, card and payment lines we code and reconcile each month.
  • Accounts to reconcileHow many bank accounts, cards and payment platforms feed the ledger.
  • Payroll footprintHeadcount and the number of states you run payroll in.
  • AR and AP volumeInvoices raised and chased, bills entered and paid.
  • Entity structureOne company, several, or a US parent with a subsidiary abroad.
  • Close cadence and reportingHow fast you need the close, and how deep the reporting goes.
  • Opening state of the booksWhether we start clean or catch up first. Catch-up is quoted separately.

Nothing here is billed by the hour. If the scope changes enough to move the fee, we tell you before the work starts, not after it is done.

Questions

The things founders ask before they sign.

What actually sets my price?

Transaction volume, how many accounts we reconcile, your payroll headcount and states, AR and AP volume, how many entities you run and how fast you need the close. We price off those, not off your revenue line, and we tell you which ones are driving your number.

Is accounting software included?

On the Seed plans, yes. The ledger is in the $99 and $149 fee, so there is no separate software bill. On the standard plans the subscriptions you already run, such as QuickBooks Online, Gusto, Ramp or Puzzle, stay on your account and are billed to you directly.

What if I am behind on my books?

We quote catch-up separately before anything starts. On Seed plans it is $49 for each past month, plus a $199 one-time setup. For heavier clean-ups we scope the work and give you a fixed number.

Are tax filings included in the monthly fee?

No, and that is deliberate. You only pay for what you actually file. State filings are $300 per state per year. Federal returns are $1,000, $1,500 or $2,500 depending on complexity, with larger structures quoted. Sales tax starts at $100 a filing and income tax support at $100 a month on Seed plans.

Can I start on Seed and move up later?

That is the intended path. Above 75 transactions a month, or once revenue passes $500,000, the standard plans take over from $300 a month. The same team carries your history across, so nothing is rebuilt.

Is there a contract or a minimum term?

No long-term lock-in. The fee is monthly and the scope is written down. If what you need changes, we re-scope and tell you the new number before the work starts.

Who does the work?

A US-based lead owns your account, reviews every close and signs it off. Execution sits with our qualified team in India, on your systems and your close calendar. We call it hybridshore: the review stays in the US, which is what keeps the quality up and the fee down.

Do you support a US company with an India subsidiary?

Yes, and it is one engagement rather than two. One team handles the US books and the Indian entity's compliance, which removes the reconciliation work between two sets of advisers who do not talk to each other.

What is not included?

Audit and assurance, legal advice, and anything requiring a licensed public accounting opinion. Payroll processing fees, software subscriptions on the standard plans, and tax filings are priced separately and listed above. Federal return pricing excludes the $200 per entity per year filing software cost.
